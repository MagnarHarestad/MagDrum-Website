BUILT AROUND DRUM SOUNDS

Find it, hear it, map it.

01 · LIBRARY

Search your sample folders

Scan WAV, AIFF and FLAC files into a local SQLite index, directly from the Windows VST3 or the standalone app. Resume interrupted scans at the next unfinished audio file, filter, audition, inspect waveforms and compare near-duplicate candidates.

02 · INSTRUMENTS

Capture plugin kits

Load an installed instrument, choose a kit, save its state and render MIDI notes at selected velocities. Previews and scan metadata join the same library, with timbral layer-change candidates to review.

03 · MIDI

Carry a pattern between kits

Build a kit on the 4×4 GM drum machine and inspect each pad’s waveform and velocity layers. The VST3 has a 1–16-bar sequencer with per-step velocity, MIDI import/export, and saved GM or custom mapping profiles.
